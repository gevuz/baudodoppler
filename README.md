# Ego Quantum

It was born from the vision of someone who dreams of creating a unique **MOBA experience**, built around distinctive, challenging mechanics for players accustomed to titles like _League of Legends_, _Dota 2_, and other games of the genre.

This GitBook serves as the foundation for the game’s **design, lore, and mechanics**, gathering early concepts, systems, and world-building ideas that will shape the essence of _Ego Quantum_.

There are already concepts for **over 30 heroes**, items, and other game elements. Everything is — and will continue to be — documented here. As the project evolves, adjustments and new ideas may emerge at any time. This early stage of development allows for full creative exploration, even if it limits us to a conceptual phase for now.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Ego Quantum does not exist as a playable game yet. It is currently a concept in development and creative exploration. If you’d like to help **bring Ego Quantum to life**, I’m looking for passionate people to collaborate and shape this universe together.
